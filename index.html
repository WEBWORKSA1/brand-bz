Frequently asked questions

Are the Brand.bz tools really free?

Yes. Every tool runs in your browser, requires no account and has no usage limit. The site is supported by advertising, sponsors and voluntary donations.

Do you store the names or data I enter into the tools?

No. Tool inputs are processed on your device. Saved favourites and palettes are kept only in your own browser's local storage.

What does the free brand audit include?

A reviewer looks at your name, positioning, visual identity, website and social presence and sends back a short written report with prioritized recommendations.

Can Brand.bz guarantee a name is available to trademark?

No. Our tools link to official databases for preliminary searches only. Always consult a qualified trademark professional before filing or launching.
