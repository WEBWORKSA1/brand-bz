Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 06, 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We also use browser local storage for some preferences.

How we use them

TypePurpose
Essential (local storage)Remember your theme, cookie choice, saved names and palettes
Advertising (Google AdSense)Serve and measure ads, which may be personalized
Embedded mediaYouTube may set cookies once you play a video

Your choices

You can accept or limit non-essential cookies using our banner, adjust your browser settings, and manage ad personalization at Google Ads Settings. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message through Google's certified consent management tools where required.
