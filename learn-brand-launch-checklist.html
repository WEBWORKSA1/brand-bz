The 40-Point Brand Launch Checklist

By the Brand.bz Editorial Team · 6 min read · Updated November 06, 2026

Use this checklist to make sure nothing important is missing on launch day. Print it, or bookmark it and tick items off as you go.

Strategy (1–8)

  1. Ideal customer profile written
  2. Customer problems and desired outcomes listed
  3. Positioning statement completed (builder)
  4. Three to five competitors analysed
  5. Core brand promise defined
  6. Mission and values drafted
  7. Pricing position chosen (budget, mid, premium)
  8. Brand story written in three short paragraphs

Name & protection (9–14)

  1. Name shortlist tested with real customers
  2. Preliminary trademark searches completed
  3. Primary domain registered
  4. Typo and alternate-extension domains considered
  5. Social handles secured
  6. Business registration aligned with brand name

Identity (15–24)

  1. Logo in full-color, one-color and reversed versions
  2. Favicon and social profile icons
  3. Color palette with HEX/RGB/CMYK values
  4. Typography choices and fallbacks
  5. Imagery and photography style
  6. Voice traits with do's and don'ts
  7. Tagline finalised
  8. One-page brand guide (builder)
  9. Email signature template
  10. Business card and invoice templates

Digital presence (25–34)

  1. Website live with clear headline and call to action
  2. About, Contact and Privacy pages published
  3. Analytics installed
  4. Business profile created on major maps and search platforms
  5. Social bios consistent everywhere
  6. Email list sign-up form working
  7. Welcome email sequence written
  8. Social post templates designed
  9. Page speed and mobile layout tested
  10. Accessibility basics checked (contrast, alt text)

Launch (35–40)

  1. Launch announcement written
  2. Friends, partners and early customers briefed
  3. Launch offer or incentive prepared
  4. Press or community outreach list built
  5. Feedback form ready
  6. 30-day post-launch review scheduled
