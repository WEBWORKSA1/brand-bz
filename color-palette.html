Using your palette

Each swatch has a role: primary, secondary, accent, light background and dark text. The label shows whether white or dark text passes WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1). Apply roughly the 60-30-10 rule: 60% neutrals, 30% secondary, 10% accent and primary for key actions.
