Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 06, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Brand.bz ("we", "us") handles information when you visit https://brand.bz.

Information we collect

  • Information you provide: when you submit a form (audit request, contact, contest entry, job application, newsletter), we receive the details you enter, such as name, email and message.
  • Tool inputs: our branding tools run in your browser. Inputs are not sent to us. Saved favourites and palettes are stored in your browser's local storage, which you can clear at any time.
  • Automatically collected data: like most websites, our hosting provider and advertising partners may collect technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and timestamps.

How we use information

To respond to inquiries, deliver audits, run contests, review applications, send newsletters you requested, improve the site and keep it secure. We do not sell your personal information.

Form processing

Form submissions are transmitted securely through a third-party form-delivery service (FormSubmit) to our inbox. Donations are processed by PayPal under PayPal's own privacy policy.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Embedded content

Videos are embedded from YouTube using privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play. Fonts are served by Google Fonts. These services may collect data under their own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or Canada's PIPEDA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your personal information. Contact us through our contact page to make a request.

Children

Brand.bz is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Data retention & security

We keep submissions only as long as needed for the purpose they were provided and use reasonable measures to protect them. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions? Use our contact form.
