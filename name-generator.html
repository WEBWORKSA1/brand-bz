How to use the brand name generator

Start with two or three words that describe your product, audience or the feeling you want to create. The generator combines them with curated word banks using five naming techniques: suffixes, prefixes, word blending, compounds and invented words. Choose Invented for highly distinctive names that are easier to trademark, or Short if you want something punchy for a domain.

Checking a name

Each card links to domain searches, social handles and the USPTO, CIPO and WIPO trademark databases. These are preliminary checks; read our 7-step naming framework before you commit.
