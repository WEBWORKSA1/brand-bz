About Brand.bz

We believe every business deserves a great brand — not just those with agency budgets.

Our mission

Brand.bz makes professional branding knowledge and tools free and accessible. We combine practical, original guides with instant browser-based tools so that anyone can go from a rough idea to a clear, consistent brand.

What we offer

  • Free tools for naming, taglines, color, typography, positioning and brand guides.
  • Original guides written in plain English by our editorial team.
  • A curated video academy featuring respected educators.
  • Free brand audits and access to a vetted talent network for hands-on help.
  • Community contests that let creatives practise and get noticed.

How we stay free

Brand.bz is supported by display advertising (Google AdSense), clearly labeled sponsorships and voluntary donations. Advertising never influences our editorial content.

Editorial standards

Guides are researched, reviewed and updated regularly. Legal topics are presented as general information only. If you spot an error, please contact us.
