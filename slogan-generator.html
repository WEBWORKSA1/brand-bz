What makes a great tagline?

A strong tagline is short (usually under eight words), communicates a benefit, and has a rhythm that makes it easy to repeat. It should complement your name rather than repeat it. Use the generated lines as raw material: swap in more specific words, read them aloud and test them with customers.

Tagline formulas that work

  • Benefit + simplicity: "Better sleep, made simple."
  • Contrast: "Less hassle. More living."
  • Invitation: "Go further with ___."
